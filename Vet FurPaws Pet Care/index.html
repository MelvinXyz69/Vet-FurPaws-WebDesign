Expert Veterinary Services for Your Pet

Microchipping

Microchipping is a safe and effective way to identify your pet. The procedure involves inserting a small chip under the skin, which contains a unique identification number.

Vaccinations

Vaccination is a crucial component of preventative healthcare for pets. It involves administering a vaccine, which stimulates the animal's immune system to produce protective antibodies against specific diseases.

Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation for pets involves a range of therapies designed to promote healing, reduce pain, and improve mobility. It can be particularly beneficial for animals recovering from surgery or injury.

Surgery

Surgery for pets may be necessary for a variety of reasons, such as to treat injuries, remove tumors, or address congenital abnormalities. Our clinic provides a range of surgical services, including spaying, neutering and orthopedic surgery.

Pet Care FAQs

How often should I take my pet to the vet?

The frequency of vet visits depends on your pet's age, breed, and health status. Generally, young pets require more frequent checkups than older, healthy pets.

What should I feed my pet?

Your pet's diet should be tailored to its age, breed, and activity level. Consult your vet for recommendations.

Can I give my pet human medicine?

No, never give your pet human medicine without consulting your veterinarian. Many human medications are toxic to pets.

How often should I groom my pet?

Grooming frequency depends on your pet's breed and coat type. Regular brushing is important for all pets.

How can I prevent fleas and ticks on my pet?

Use preventative medications as recommended by your vet and maintain a clean environment.

What should I do if my pet is injured or becomes sick?

Contact your veterinarian immediately.
